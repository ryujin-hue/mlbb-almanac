Name

confirmed
Name
MLBB Almanac — short form the Almanac
Why
An almanac is a reference you consult for the current reading — a table of tides gives today's tide and every tide before it. That is exactly the product: the archive and today's meta are the same table, and the live entry is the one you read.
Not
Not a magazine, not a guide, not a "hub". A published reference that changes on a schedule.
Spoken
"Check the Almanac" — works in a ranked lobby, a Discord channel and a pull request.
Replaces
MLBB Meta Center (live) and MLBB Historical Database (archive)

04

Logo

mark, wordmark, construction, prompt
PICKED
Mark — "the almanac table"

Twelve dashes in three columns and four rows — a page of tabulated entries. One cell is lit: the last in reading order, which is the current one. The archive is the grid; the live entry is the only cyan.

Wordmark lockup
MLBBALMANAC

MLBB in 500 weight, slate, +0.16em tracking — a label on the data. ALMANAC in 700 weight, near-white, +0.012em — the thing itself. Gap 0.40em.

Favicon / app icon

Same 12-cell art on a #0B0F1A rounded square, 32-unit box, cells at 1u inset. The lit cell keeps full-bleed width — do not inset it.

Construction

  • Grid24 × 24 units. 3 columns at x = 2, 9, 16. 4 rows at y = 3, 8, 13, 18. Cell 6 × 2.5u. Column gap 1u, row gap 2.5u — cells bind horizontally into rows, which is what makes it read as a table rather than a grid of blocks.
  • Lit cellRow 4, column 3 — the last cell in reading order, so it means "the current entry". Never move it to the middle; a lit middle cell reads as "selected", not "now".
  • ColourDark surface: cells #2F3C57, lit cell #00E5FF. Light surface: cells #2F3C57, lit cell #007C8E (4.5:1 on #F4F6FA). Print single-colour: all #0B0F1A. Gold never appears in the mark — it is reserved for patch tags and verified-source marks.
  • Minimum size16px digital. Below 16px, drop the middle two rows and keep two rows of three.
  • Clear space1u on all sides, measured from the outer cell edges.
  • MotionOn refresh, the lit cell steps from its old position to the new last cell over 180ms; the grid does not move. No pulse, no glow, no shimmer.
  • Rejected4×4 squares (mushes below 24px, reads as a QR code) · 2×2 blocks (reads as an app grid) · vertical stack (reads as a list icon) · a lit middle cell (loses "now") · a fully lit row (reads as a progress bar).
  • ForbiddenNo gradients, no glow, no rounded corners, no drop shadows, no hero silhouettes, no shield, no crown, no hexagon, no compass rose, no book illustration.

Image-generation prompt

MARK — flat vector logo on a solid #0B0F1A background.
A grid of twelve short horizontal rectangular dashes, arranged in three columns
and four rows, evenly spaced. Eleven dashes are flat desaturated slate blue
#2F3C57. The single dash in the bottom-right position is flat electric cyan
#00E5FF. Each dash is 6 units wide and 2.5 units tall on a 24-unit square
canvas; column gap 1 unit, row gap 2.5 units; square corners, no outlines,
no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no text. Centred with even margins.
Geometric, minimal, high information density, reference-book aesthetic.

WORDMARK LOCKUP — same background and same 12-dash mark on the left, followed
by the text "MLBB ALMANAC" in a clean geometric sans-serif, all caps. "MLBB" in
medium weight slate blue #2F3C57 with wide letter spacing, "ALMANAC" in bold

FAVICON — the 12-dash mark centred on a #0B0F1A rounded square with 14 percent

Production art is the SVG in assets/ — it is the only version with exact hex values and a legible wordmark. Use the prompt for exploration, social avatars and anything a raster generator has to produce.

05

Brand personality

four words, each testable
01
Tabulated
Everything lands in a table: comparable, sortable, quotable. If a claim cannot be put in a column, it does not go in.
02
Current
Refreshed at 02:00 UTC and timestamped on every screen. Stale or offline is stated, not hidden behind a cached number.
03
Sourced
Every figure links back to where it came from. Where there is no source, the API returns available: false rather than a number.
04
Unadorned
No verdicts, no framing, no adjectives. A patch with no hero data is listed as a patch with no hero data.
