Name

Name
MLBB Almanac — short form the Almanac
Why
An almanac is a reference you consult for the current reading — a table of tides gives today's tide and every tide before it. That is exactly the product: the archive and today's meta are the same table, and the live entry is the one you read.
Not
Not a magazine, not a guide, not a "hub". A published reference that changes on a schedule.
Spoken
"Check the Almanac" — works in a ranked lobby, a Discord channel and a pull request.
Replaces
MLBB Meta Center (live) and MLBB Historical Database (archive)

04

Logo

origami wordmark, icon, construction
PICKED
Wordmark — origami "MLBB-A"

Folded-paper letterforms in gold and silver. The lockup carries "MLBB" and "Almanac" (A) in one line, so no separate wordmark text is needed beside it. This is the shape in the site header.

Icon — "the almanac table"
MLBBALMANAC

Twelve dashes, one lit. Too small for the origami at 16px, so the mark stays the icon: favicon, PWA icon, app tile. Never the headline lockup.

Favicon / app icon

Same 12-cell art on a #0B0F1A rounded square, 32-unit box, cells at 1u inset. The lit cell keeps full-bleed width — do not inset it.

Construction

  • WordmarkOrigami, 800 × 240 viewBox, letters at a 110-unit cap height, group translated to (85, 65). Folded from flat polygons — no strokes, no fills with gradients. The shadow is one feDropShadow on the whole group (dx 4, dy 8, blur 6), never per polygon.
  • Two formsThe origami wordmark is the lockup and appears in the site header. The 12-cell mark below is the icon only — favicon, PWA icon, app tile — because origami does not survive 16px.
  • Gold and silverThe wordmark carries its own ramp (#f3c65f / #d49b27 / #b47b14 and #f8fafc / #e2e8f0 / #cbd5e1 / #94a3b8), separate from the UI tokens. Cyan #00E5FF and gold #F5C518 mean live data and tier, and stay out of the logo.
  • Grid24 × 24 units. 3 columns at x = 2, 9, 16. 4 rows at y = 3, 8, 13, 18. Cell 6 × 2.5u. Column gap 1u, row gap 2.5u — cells bind horizontally into rows, which is what makes it read as a table rather than a grid of blocks.
  • Lit cellRow 4, column 3 — the last cell in reading order, so it means "the current entry". Never move it to the middle; a lit middle cell reads as "selected", not "now".
  • Icon colourDark surface: cells #2F3C57, lit cell #00E5FF. Light surface: cells #2F3C57, lit cell #007C8E (4.5:1 on #F4F6FA). Print single-colour: all #0B0F1A. Gold never appears in the mark — it is reserved for patch tags and verified-source marks.
  • Minimum sizeIcon: 16px digital. Wordmark: 56px — below that the origami folds stop reading. Below 16px, drop the middle two rows and keep two rows of three.
  • Clear space1u on all sides, measured from the outer cell edges.
  • MotionOn refresh, the lit cell steps from its old position to the new last cell over 180ms; the grid does not move. No pulse, no glow, no shimmer.
  • Rejected4×4 squares (mushes below 24px, reads as a QR code) · 2×2 blocks (reads as an app grid) · vertical stack (reads as a list icon) · a lit middle cell (loses "now") · a fully lit row (reads as a progress bar).
  • Forbidden (icon)No gradients, no glow, no rounded corners, no drop shadows, no hero silhouettes, no shield, no crown, no hexagon, no compass rose, no book illustration.

05

Brand personality

four words, each testable
01
Tabulated
Everything lands in a table: comparable, sortable, quotable. If a claim cannot be put in a column, it does not go in.
02
Current
Refreshed at 02:00 UTC and timestamped on every screen. Stale or offline is stated, not hidden behind a cached number.
03
Sourced
Every figure links back to where it came from. Where there is no source, the API returns available: false rather than a number.
04
Unadorned
No verdicts, no framing, no adjectives. A patch with no hero data is listed as a patch with no hero data.
